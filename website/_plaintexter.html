Plaintexter

Download for Mac ↓
  • Open the download, drag Plaintexter to Applications, and launch it.
  • Copy something. Click pt in the menu bar, and your clipboard contents will be converted to plain text.
  • Right-click pt to choose Plain Text or Markdown, undo a conversion, or enable Open at Launch (start at login).

Plain Text removes styling. Markdown keeps supported formatting from rich text, including bold, italics, links, and lists.

Images and scanned PDF pages are read with Apple’s on-device OCR. OCR extracts text, not its styling. If an image has no substantial readable text, the result is “No text found to OCR.”

Clipboard content stays on your Mac.
